# Ujobs — Brand Film Script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Ujobs turns a local need into a clear, trusted service experience.
- **Identity:** Ujobs navy, turquoise, cream, and warm amber; Arabic-first type with a compact Latin wordmark.
- **Hero:** A glowing service request card that grows into a network, then resolves into a confident accepted booking.
- **Motion:** Snap-pop kinetic type, node-to-card network lines, radial aperture reveals, and compound scale-through transitions.
- **Arc:** Start with the friction of searching alone, accelerate through discovery and comparison, slow briefly on trust and the fixed 10 د.م commission, then finish with a clean brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The final scene inverts the opening dark field and gathers the network, request, and trust marks into one centered Ujobs signature.
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds, five 4.8-second scenes.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.0–04.8s — «من أين أبدأ؟»

Purpose: Name the everyday service need without showing a generic title card.

Exact copy: `تحتاج لخدمة كيف ما كانت؟` appears at 00.35s; `ابدأ من هنا.` appears at 01.05s; supporting line `أقرب مما تتوقع.` appears at 01.7s; small label `خدمة محلية بثقة` appears at 02.0s and holds through 04.1s.

Composition and artistic idea: A turquoise dot enters from the upper edge and leaves a thin route line behind it. The line bends into a rounded request card in the lower half. Three small field labels pop into the card one at a time: `الخدمة`, `المدينة`, `الوقت`.

Choreography: 00.0–00.5s dot snaps in; 00.45–01.0s route line draws; 00.7–01.4s headline resolves in two staggered lines; 01.5–02.7s card scales from 0.86 to 1.03 and settles while its three fields pop at 180ms intervals; 03.2–04.1s the dot makes one short orbit around the card; 04.1–04.8s card pushes toward the lens with blur and the turquoise dot remains visible as the carrier.

Transition: The card's rounded rectangle expands beyond the frame; its top-left corner becomes the circular network field in Shot 02.

Assets and technique: CSS card, SVG route line, deterministic circles, Framer Motion spring and scale-through.

Sound intent: Music begins with a crisp, warm percussive pulse; beat alignment remains unverified until the music bed exists.

### Shot 02 / 04.8–09.6s — «الأقرب إليك»

Purpose: Show the marketplace as a living local network, not an anonymous directory.

Exact copy: `مهنيون موثّقون` appears at 05.2s; `في مدينتك` appears at 06.0s; node labels `كهرباء`, `تنظيف`, `تصميم` appear at 06.5s, 07.0s, and 07.5s.

Composition and artistic idea: The outgoing card corner becomes a large pale circular map plate. A central U mark pulses, and three orbiting nodes draw lines back to it. A location pin grows from the lower node and is carried into the next shot.

Choreography: 04.8–05.4s map plate opens through a radial aperture; 05.2–06.0s headline splits across the upper safe zone; 06.1–07.3s nodes pop with stiff springs and connection strokes trace one at a time; 07.5–08.5s a pin rises from the lower node and briefly enlarges; 08.5–09.6s camera pushes through the pin while the network lines shear outward.

Transition: The pin's inner circle matches the check ring in Shot 03.

Assets and technique: CSS map plate, SVG paths and pin, staggered motion, no external image.

Sound intent: Add a brighter rhythmic layer under the existing pulse; no voiceover.

### Shot 03 / 09.6–14.4s — «اطلب. قارن. اختر.»

Purpose: Explain the customer flow as three visible decisions.

Exact copy: `اطلب.` at 10.0s; `قارن.` at 10.8s; `اختر.` at 11.6s; card labels `تفاصيل واضحة`, `عروض متعددة`, `اختيارك` appear at 10.3s, 11.1s, and 12.0s.

Composition and artistic idea: A tall phone-like card is already partially visible on entry. The three verbs replace each other in the upper third while the card contents transform below them: blank fields become offer rows, then a confirmed booking.

Choreography: 09.6–10.0s pin circle opens behind the card; 10.0–11.0s first verb and two request fields appear; 11.0–12.0s two offer rows snap into place and the headline rotates to the second verb; 12.0–13.0s selected row brightens and the third verb locks; 13.0–14.0s a check ring draws around the selection while the card tilts 2 degrees; 14.0–14.4s the check ring scales through the frame.

Transition: The check ring is the exact carrier for the trust seal in Shot 04.

Assets and technique: CSS phone card, rows, check ring, masked word replacement, controlled tilt.

Sound intent: Three short percussive accents align with the three verbs when the bed supports it; alignment must be checked, not assumed.

### Shot 04 / 14.4–19.2s — «واضح وعادل»

Purpose: Make the platform's trust promise concrete and show the fixed commission without overclaiming bank connectivity.

Exact copy: `واضح وعادل` at 14.8s; large `10 د.م` at 15.7s; `عند قبول الطلب المؤهل` at 16.5s; `دعم محلي` at 17.3s.

Composition and artistic idea: On a cream field, the incoming check ring becomes a stamped circular seal. The 10 د.م amount is set oversized inside it. Two small support marks trace in from opposite sides, establishing clarity and local help.

Choreography: 14.4–15.0s ring opens into cream; 15.0–15.8s title slides on a masked baseline; 15.6–16.5s 10 د.م scales from 0.7 with one controlled overshoot; 16.6–17.5s the supporting line and two check marks trace in; 17.8–18.6s the seal rotates 3 degrees and settles; 18.6–19.2s seal collapses to a turquoise dot at frame center.

Transition: The dot becomes the accent on the U mark in the final lockup.

Assets and technique: Radial ring, stamped seal, line-drawn checks, cream/navy contrast.

Sound intent: Music briefly opens up and leaves a clean space around the amount.

### Shot 05 / 19.2–24.0s — «خدمات محلية بثقة»

Purpose: Resolve the product promise in a memorable, readable end card.

Exact copy: `أطلب` at 19.45s; `قارن` at 20.1s; `أنجز` at 20.75s; `ujobs` at 21.7s; `خدمات محلية بثقة` at 22.4s.

Composition and artistic idea: Three action words arrive as large vertical punches and compress into a centered U mark. The mark becomes the letterform-like anchor of the Ujobs name while the turquoise dot returns to the same position as Shot 01.

Choreography: 19.2–21.2s action words punch in with alternating vertical offsets; 21.2–22.0s words compress toward the center, leaving a turquoise dot and a short light trail; 21.7–22.6s `ujobs` resolves with a masked reveal; 22.5–23.8s tagline and three small trust dots settle; 23.8–24.0s the dot drifts one frame toward the opening position to make the loop handoff intentional.

Transition: The final turquoise dot aligns with Shot 01's incoming dot. The fields invert from navy to navy, so the loop is a match on position rather than a blank fade.

Assets and technique: Large kinetic type, centered lockup, no CTA button, no interactive elements.

Sound intent: Music resolves on a warm, short tonal lift; loop handoff should be clean and unvoiced.
